Lenz's Law Core Rule

"The direction of the induced current is always such as to oppose the change in magnetic flux that produces it."

N-pole moves INTO coil: Facing end becomes North (Repulsion).
N-pole moves AWAY from coil: Facing end becomes South (Attraction).
Stationary magnet: Flux is constant $\frac{d\Phi}{dt} = 0 \implies \mathcal{E} = 0$.
